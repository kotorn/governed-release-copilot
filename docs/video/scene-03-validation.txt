ReleaseChangeV1 gives every change the same evidence shape: source, component, business reason, before and after artifacts, test results, impact, risk, rollback, and changeHash. Before any downstream action, the Python validator checks Draft 2020-12 schema rules and recomputes the canonical SHA-256 hash. Changing the release ID or timestamp does not change the material hash. Changing the business evidence does. A known hash is returned as Duplicate before drafting begins.
